# Supply Agreement C03

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Keystone MRO Supply ("Supplier")
**Category:** MRO

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on January 1, 2025 and remains in force until December 31, 2027 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

## 2. Pricing
Supplier will invoice the following firm prices for the full term. Prices exclude sales tax.

| Item code | Description | Unit | Unit price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MRO-114 | Nitrile gloves, box of 100 | box | 11.40 |
| MRO-207 | Hydraulic oil ISO 46, 5 gal | pail | 64.50 |
| MRO-311 | Ball bearing 6205-2RS | each | 7.85 |
| MRO-415 | V-belt B48 | each | 18.20 |

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 30 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
No volume rebate applies to this Agreement.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its MRO requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
