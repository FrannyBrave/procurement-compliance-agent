# Supply Agreement C07

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and CloudPeak Software ("Supplier")
**Category:** IT & Software

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on February 1, 2025 and remains in force until January 31, 2028 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

## 2. Pricing
Supplier will invoice the following firm prices for the full term. Prices exclude sales tax.

| Item code | Description | Unit | Unit price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT-701 | ERP user license, monthly | seat | 85.00 |
| IT-705 | Premium support add-on, monthly | month | 1,800.00 |

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 60 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
No volume rebate applies to this Agreement.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its IT & Software requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
