# Supply Agreement C06

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Harbor Logistics Group ("Supplier")
**Category:** Logistics & Freight

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on September 1, 2025 and remains in force until August 31, 2027 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

## 2. Pricing
Prices are fixed for the full term and exclude sales tax. Supplier agrees to the following:
- Item LOG-601 (ocean container 40ft, shanghai to long beach) will be supplied at $3,900.00 per container.
- Item LOG-610 (drayage, long beach port to dc) will be supplied at $640.00 per move.

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
For each calendar quarter in which Buyer's purchases under this Agreement exceed $80,000, Supplier shall credit Buyer a rebate equal to 1.5% of that quarter's total purchases. Supplier shall issue the credit within 30 days after the end of the quarter.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its Logistics & Freight requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
