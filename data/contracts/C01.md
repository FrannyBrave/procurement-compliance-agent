# Supply Agreement C01

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Apex Industrial Metals ("Supplier")
**Category:** Direct Materials

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on July 1, 2025 and remains in force until June 30, 2027 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

## 2. Pricing
Supplier will invoice the following firm prices for the full term. Prices exclude sales tax.

| Item code | Description | Unit | Unit price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DM-1010 | Cold-rolled steel sheet, 16 gauge | ton | 1,185.00 |
| DM-1022 | Aluminum bar stock 6061, 2 in | ft | 14.25 |
| DM-1031 | Stainless tube 304, 1.5 in | ft | 22.80 |

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
For each calendar quarter in which Buyer's purchases under this Agreement exceed $150,000, Supplier shall credit Buyer a rebate equal to 2% of that quarter's total purchases. Supplier shall issue the credit within 30 days after the end of the quarter.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its Direct Materials requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
