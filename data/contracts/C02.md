# Supply Agreement C02

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Northline Polymers ("Supplier")
**Category:** Direct Materials

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on October 1, 2025 and remains in force until September 30, 2027 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

## 2. Pricing
Prices are fixed for the full term and exclude sales tax. Supplier agrees to the following:
- Item DM-2001 (hdpe resin pellets) will be supplied at $1.92 per kg.
- Item DM-2014 (polycarbonate sheet 4x8, 1/4 in) will be supplied at $168.00 per sheet.

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 30 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
For each calendar quarter in which Buyer's purchases under this Agreement exceed $60,000, Supplier shall credit Buyer a rebate equal to 3% of that quarter's total purchases. Supplier shall issue the credit within 30 days after the end of the quarter.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its Direct Materials requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
