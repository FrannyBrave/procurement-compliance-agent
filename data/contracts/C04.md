# Supply Agreement C04

**Between:** Granite Ridge Manufacturing, Inc. ("Buyer") and Brightway Facility Services ("Supplier")
**Category:** Facilities

## 1. Term
This Agreement takes effect on July 1, 2024 and remains in force until June 30, 2026 (the "Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier under Section 7.
Renewal is not automatic. Any extension must be signed by both parties before the expiration date.

## 2. Pricing
Prices are fixed for the full term and exclude sales tax. Supplier agrees to the following:
- Item FAC-100 (janitorial service, plant a (monthly)) will be supplied at $9,400.00 per month.
- Item FAC-110 (hvac preventive maintenance visit) will be supplied at $1,250.00 per visit.

Any price change requires a written amendment signed by Buyer's Procurement Director. Invoices above the contracted unit price will be short-paid to the contracted price.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due net 30 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Each invoice must reference a valid Buyer purchase order number and must not exceed the PO quantity or price.

## 4. Volume Rebate
No volume rebate applies to this Agreement.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance
Goods are delivered FOB destination. Buyer may reject non-conforming goods within 10 business days.

## 6. Exclusivity
Buyer will source its Facilities requirements for the items listed in Section 2 from Supplier for the term, except in an emergency approved in writing by Procurement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured.
